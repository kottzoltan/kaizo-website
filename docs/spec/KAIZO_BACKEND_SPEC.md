# KAIZO Product Specification

## Vision

KAIZO is a modular Business Operating System for SMEs.

It combines:

- CRM
- Calendar & Booking
- Tasks
- Proposals & Contracts
- Billing integrations
- AI Chat Agents
- AI Voice Agents
- Email Assistant
- Marketing Content Studio

into a single platform.

The system must work even without AI modules.

AI modules are optional add-ons.

---

## Target Market

- SMEs
- Service companies
- Salons
- Sport centers
- Healthcare providers
- Consultants
- Agencies
- Professional services

---

## Core Principles

- Modular
- AI-first but not AI-dependent
- SaaS multi-tenant
- White-label capable
- API-first
- Mobile friendly
- No mandatory ERP dependency
